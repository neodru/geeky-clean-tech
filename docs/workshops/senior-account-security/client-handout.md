# Your Accounts, Made Safer and Simpler

_A take-home guide from Geeky Clean Technology_

## What we did today

1. **You have a new Gmail address.** It's now your main email and the "master key" for your Apple, Google, and Microsoft accounts. Your old Cox email still works. Its messages are forwarded to Gmail automatically, so you won't miss anything.
2. **Your face (or fingerprint) is your password.** Your iPhone unlocks with your face, and your computer unlocks with your face, fingerprint, or a PIN. Most websites will now fill in your password after a glance at your phone.
3. **You have "passkeys."** A passkey is a lock that only opens for _your_ face on _your_ phone or computer. Scammers can't trick you into giving it away, because there is nothing to type or say.
4. **Your phone number is your backup key.** If you ever get locked out, a text to your phone helps you get back in.
5. **You have a Recovery Card.** It's your emergency backup, kept somewhere safe at home.

## The 3 Scam Rules

1. **Real companies never call you asking for a code, a password, or to "connect to your computer."** Hang up.
2. **A code or prompt you didn't ask for means someone else is trying to get in.** Don't share it. Don't tap "Yes."
3. **A pop-up warning with a phone number is always fake.** Close it and call us.

> When in doubt, **hang up and call Geeky Clean Technology** or your family contact. We will never be upset that you called.
